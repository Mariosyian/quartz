## Kakijun (Stroke Order)

A custom made educational website that uses the [KanjiVG](https://kanjivg.tagaini.net/) stroke order information, as well as [KANJIDIC2](https://www.edrdg.org/kanjidic/kanjd2index_legacy.html) and [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/jmdict/j_jmdict.html) for definitions and common word frequency statistical data.

Hosted: https://kakijun-17408e322e1b.herokuapp.com/

DISCLAIMERS:
1. This web app was created with LLM assistance
1. This web app does **NOT** save any information outside of your own browser. The stroke order + accuracy judging, as well as the progress saving is all handled by your own browser.
