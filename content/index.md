## Introduction

Welcome to the beginning of your Japanese learning journey!

These are notes that [I](https://github.com/mariosyian) have collected and created throughout my learning journey. They are by no means extensive, and some have never been proof-read. If you have noticed anything missing or outright wrong, feel free to report it under the [Issues](https://github.com/Mariosyian/quartz/issues) tab of the [official repository](https://github.com/Mariosyian/quartz).

## How to use these notes

A lot of the notes are standalone. What this means is that they are an isolated page of knowledge that may, or may not, necessarilly link to something and can be studied at its own pace. No pre-requisites, unless (potentially) stated otherwise. Make use of the graph view on the right side of the page to notice which page is connected to how many, and which, other pages. Each connection is represented by a hyperlink on that page.

All materials can be found on the tabs of the left and are in no particular order, unless numbered as such. Please note that some of the dropdown menus also contain an introductory page in themselves, so try to click on the actual text of the dropdown first, as opposed to just the arrow itself.

There is technically no full-proof way of learning a language. As long as you know how to read the alphabet, the rest is understanding sentence structures, tenses, and the different nuances each language has unique to itself. Beyond that, it's just vocabulary really. Having said that, I'm not a qualified teacher so I can not provide you with a roadmap of where to start, or how to carry on.
